# Related projects

These projects provide other Quran resources. Some have licensing restrictions that prevent inclusion here; others have not yet been integrated.

## QUL (Quranic Universal Library)

The creators of Tarteel AI provide a valuable collection of Quran resources for developers:

https://qul.tarteel.ai/resources

QUL describes it as the largest online collection of downloadable Quran content. Many of these resources could eventually be integrated into Ghoran.

## Hablullah

The Hablullah team maintains Quran resources for developers:

https://github.com/hablullah/data-quran

Its licensing restrictions are one reason we have not used the repository. The projects also have different goals: Hablullah collects and normalizes Quran data, while Ghoran aims to provide a JavaScript framework for working with it.
